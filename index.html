Questions people actually ask

Straight answers, starting with the uncomfortable one

Is selling to Patriot Home Buyer a scam? How do I know you're legitimate?

Fair question — this category earned its reputation. Don't take our word for it: look up our purchases at the county clerk, ask for proof of funds before you tell us anything, and notice that every closing happens at [TITLE COMPANY], not with us. If any of that doesn't check out, walk away.

How do you determine the cash offer?

Comparable sales for the fixed-up value, minus an itemized repair estimate, minus our holding, resale and closing costs, minus our margin. Every line is on the offer sheet.

Will the price drop after inspection?

No. We inspect before we write the number, and the Price Lock addendum says the price doesn't change for condition after you sign.

Are there any fees, commissions or closing costs?

None. We pay the standard seller-side closing costs. The number on the contract is the number on the settlement statement.

Do I need to make repairs or clean?

No. Leave the repairs. Leave the furniture. Leave anything you don't want to take.

How fast can you close?

As fast as [X] days once title is clear. Oklahoma title work usually takes 2–3 weeks — anyone promising 7 days on every house should explain what happens when the title search finds something.

Do I have to accept right away?

No. Take three days, take three weeks. We won't call you in between unless you ask us to.

I inherited a house and probate is still open. Can you buy it?

Yes, and it's one of the situations we handle most. We work with your probate attorney and the title company, and can write the contract subject to court confirmation where Oklahoma requires it. Fair warning: in a regular probate, a private sale generally must be confirmed at 90% or more of appraised value, so we'll tell you early if that limits the price.

I live out of state. Do I have to fly back?

No. We handle fully remote closings — e-signatures, electronic notarization where permitted, and overnight document delivery through the title company.

What if I'm behind on payments or facing foreclosure?

We can close before the sale date and pay the arrears and liens through the title company at closing, so you see every number first. We do not stop foreclosures or negotiate with your lender — if you want to keep the house, talk to a HUD-approved housing counselor.

I have tenants. Do I have to evict them first?

No. We buy with tenants in place, honor the existing lease and take over as the new landlord.

Back taxes, liens, code violations?

All normal. They're paid at closing out of the proceeds, and you'll see every line on the settlement statement before you sign.

Can I stay in the house for a while after closing?

Usually yes. We put the dates in writing so you're not moving twice.

Will you spam me if I ask for an offer?

No. One conversation, one written number. We follow up only if you ask, and we never sell or share your information.

What if I change my mind?

Cancel any time before closing. No fee, no argument.
